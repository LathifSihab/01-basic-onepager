## Note on the About photo

The spec (§3, §5.4) asks for a portrait of the grower. Commons has no suitably licensed
portrait of a Dutch bulb grower, and using a real, identifiable stranger's face to stand in
for a fictional fourth-generation family would misrepresent that person. `portret.webp` is
therefore a field at Hillegom rather than a face. If the client wants a person there, that
is a photoshoot or a paid stock licence.
